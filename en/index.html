FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is Nile Stone Café?

20 El-Malek El-Saleh St., Manial El-Roda, Old Cairo, Cairo. The café is a garden right on the Nile.

What are Nile Stone's opening hours?

Open every day from 3 PM until 3 AM.

How much is a romantic date at Nile Stone?

A romantic date for two starts from 800 EGP. It includes a decorated Nile-side table with candles and flowers, plus dinner. The final price depends on the extras you choose.

How much do events (engagement, katb el-ketab, birthday) cost?

Events start from 2,500 EGP. Tell us what you need (guest count, food, decor, DJ, photography) and we'll send you an exact quote.

How many guests can the venue host?

Up to 40 guests, and the whole venue is closed privately for your event.

What is the Girls' Night at Nile Stone?

A ladies-only night held once a month, with Zumba, karaoke and a nostalgia party. Tickets are 200 EGP. Message us to find out the next date.

Can I fish at Nile Stone?

Yes! The garden sits right on the Nile, so bring your fishing rod and fish while you hang out.

How do I book?

WhatsApp us on +20 109 319 5894, fill in the booking form on this site (it sends a ready-made WhatsApp message), or DM us on Instagram.
