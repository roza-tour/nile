FAQ

Nile Stone Café FAQ

Everything you need to know about Nile Stone: location, hours, prices and booking.

The place

Where is Nile Stone Café?

20 El-Malek El-Saleh St., Manial El-Roda, Old Cairo, Cairo. The café is a garden right on the Nile.

What are Nile Stone's opening hours?

Open every day from 3 PM until 3 AM.

Is the café really on the Nile?

Yes, seating is in an open garden directly on the Nile, facing the corniche lights at night.

Can I fish at Nile Stone?

Yes! The garden sits right on the Nile, so bring your fishing rod and fish while you hang out.

How do I book?

WhatsApp us on +20 109 319 5894, fill in the booking form on this site (it sends a ready-made WhatsApp message), or DM us on Instagram.

Menu & prices

What are drink prices at Nile Stone?

Prices range from 15 EGP (water) to 140 EGP. Turkish coffee is 55, mojitos start at 90, smoothies at 80 and fresh juices at 60.

Do you serve shisha?

Yes. Fruit shisha is 100 EGP, mixed fruit is 120 EGP, and a disposable hygienic hose is available for 20 EGP.

Does Nile Stone serve food?

Food and open buffets are served for events and group dinners as part of the package. The everyday menu is hot drinks, juices, mojitos, smoothies, milkshakes and shisha.

Romantic dates

How much is a romantic date at Nile Stone?

A romantic date for two starts from 800 EGP. It includes a decorated Nile-side table with candles and flowers, plus dinner. The final price depends on the extras you choose.

Can I choose the date decor color?

Yes. Pick your color (purple, red, white & gold, pink or blue) and we'll set it up.

Can you help with a surprise or proposal?

Yes. Share the idea on WhatsApp and we'll arrange it: flowers, cake, a special song, or any detail you like.

Private events

How much do events (engagement, katb el-ketab, birthday) cost?

Events start from 2,500 EGP. Tell us what you need (guest count, food, decor, DJ, photography) and we'll send you an exact quote.

What's included in an event package?

Full decor, food and open buffet, cake, a photographer, DJ and sound, and drinks, all tailored to your request.

How many guests can the venue host?

Up to 40 guests, and the whole venue is closed privately for your event.

Is the venue private during events?

Yes. For private events the entire café is closed just for you.

Is it good for a small engagement?

Yes. It suits an intimate family engagement of up to 40 guests, with the whole venue closed for you.

Can I hold my katb el-ketab at Nile Stone?

Yes. We set up a calm Nile-side ceremony, followed by a family celebration, for up to 40 guests. Packages start from 2,500 EGP.

Girls' Night

What is the Girls' Night at Nile Stone?

A ladies-only night held once a month, with Zumba, karaoke and a nostalgia party. Tickets are 200 EGP. Message us to find out the next date.

When is the next Girls' Night?

It runs monthly. Message us on WhatsApp (+20 109 319 5894) or Instagram and we'll tell you the nearest date.

Is it ladies only?

Yes, Girls' Night is for women only.
